Our Story

Since Our Beginning

Where Hospitality
Feels Like Home

Shreeji Hotel & Banquet was born from a simple vision — to create a place in Karnal where every guest feels genuinely welcome, whether they're staying for a night, celebrating a milestone, or simply dropping by for a good meal.

Over the years, we've grown into a complete hospitality destination — offering A.C. rooms, grand banquet halls, a warm restaurant, and a refreshing swimming pool. Every corner of Shreeji is designed with care, and every guest is treated like family.

Located at Kalpana Chawla Dwar on Munak Road, we're easy to find, easy to reach, and easy to love. Come once — and you'll know why our guests keep coming back.

— Team Shreeji
